Meaning of Variables in the W (Environmental Variables)

ALLSKY_SFC_LW_DWN_mean - Mean downward longwave (thermal infrared) radiation reaching the land surface.
ALLSKY_SFC_PAR_TOT_mean - Mean total photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) reaching the surface.
ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DNI_mean - Mean direct-normal shortwave solar radiation.
ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN_mean - Mean total downward shortwave solar radiation reaching the surface.
ALLSKY_SFC_UVA_mean - Mean ultraviolet-A (UVA; approximately 315-400 nm) radiation reaching the surface.
ALLSKY_SFC_UVB_mean - Mean ultraviolet-B (UVB; approximately 280-315 nm) radiation reaching the surface.
ETP_mean - Mean potential evapotranspiration estimated from the environmental data.
EVPTRNS_mean - Mean evapotranspiration-related flux.
FROST_DAYS_mean - Mean frost-day indicator over the environmental period; when summarized using the mean, this represents the proportion of days classified as frost days.
FRUE_mean - Mean temperature response factor for radiation-use efficiency.
GDD_mean - Mean daily growing degree accumulation calculated using the specified cardinal temperatures.
GWETROOT_mean - Mean root-zone soil wetness, representing moisture conditions in the deeper soil profile.
GWETTOP_mean - Mean near-surface soil wetness, representing moisture conditions in the upper soil layer.
n_mean - Mean estimated actual duration of sunshine.
N_mean - Mean astronomical daylight duration (photoperiod).
P_ETP_mean - Mean precipitation-evapotranspiration water-balance variable.
PAR_TEMP_mean - Mean ratio or relationship between photosynthetically active radiation and temperature generated by EnvRtype.
PETP_mean - Mean precipitation minus estimated potential evapotranspiration.
PRECTOT_mean - Mean daily precipitation.
QV2M_mean - Mean specific humidity at 2 m above the surface.
RH2M_mean - Mean relative humidity at 2 m above the surface.
RTA_mean - Mean extraterrestrial radiation at the top of the atmosphere.
SPV_mean - Mean slope of the saturation vapor-pressure curve.
T2M_MAX_mean - Mean of the daily maximum air temperatures at 2 m.
T2M_mean - Mean air temperature at 2 m.
T2M_MIN_mean - Mean of the daily minimum air temperatures at 2 m.
T2M_RANGE_mean - Mean daily temperature range, calculated as maximum temperature minus minimum temperature.
T2MDEW_mean - Mean dew-point or frost-point temperature at 2 m.
TH1_mean - Mean temperature-humidity index calculated using the first EnvRtype formulation.
TH2_mean - Mean temperature-humidity index calculated using the second EnvRtype formulation.
VPD_mean - Mean vapor-pressure deficit.
WS2M_mean - Mean wind speed at 2 m above the surface.
Chilling_Hrs - Total number of hourly temperature observations at or below 7.2 degrees C during the specified environmental period.
Chill_Unit - Accumulated positive Utah-type chilling units calculated from hourly temperatures.
Freeze_Event - Number of hourly temperature observations at or below 0 degrees C. In the current code, this is more accurately interpreted as freeze hours rather than the number of independent freeze events.
Chill_Portions - Accumulated chill portions calculated using the Dynamic Model.
Temp_diff - Mean day-night temperature contrast calculated using the custom daynight_hours() function.
Day_Temp - Mean daytime temperature calculated from the designated daytime hours in the custom daynight_hours() function.
